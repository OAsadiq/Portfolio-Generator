For landlords

Hand us the keys. Stop chasing rent.

Two ways to work with us, depending on how involved you want to be. Both mean your property is looked after and your evenings are your own.

Full Property Management

We find and vet tenants, collect rent, handle repairs and inspections, and keep you legal. You get a statement and your money. That's it.

Rent-to-Rent Guaranteed rent

We take your property on a fixed agreement and pay you a guaranteed rent every month, voids or not. No tenant hassle, no surprise bills. Just a number you can count on.
