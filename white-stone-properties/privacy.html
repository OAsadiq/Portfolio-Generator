Privacy policy

White Stone Properties (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal information you share with us. We handle it in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. If you have any questions, email admin@whitestonepropertyandestates.com.

What we collect and why

We only ask for what we need to help you. Depending on how you get in touch, that may include:

  • Enquiries: your name, email, phone and message, so we can reply and arrange viewings or management discussions.
  • Tenant applications: in addition to contact details, your preferred move-in date and employment information, so we can assess and progress an application. This may later involve referencing and right-to-rent checks.
  • Short stay bookings: the dates you request and contact details, so we can confirm availability.

Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent when you submit a form, and on legitimate interest and the steps needed to enter into a contract when we act on an enquiry or application. Where the law requires checks (for example right-to-rent), we rely on legal obligation.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application data only as long as needed for the purpose it was given, and to meet legal and tax obligations, then delete it. [Confirm your retention period, e.g. 12 months for unsuccessful applicants.]

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only where necessary: with landlords (for applications), referencing or deposit-protection providers, and service providers who host this site or process forms on our behalf. [List any third parties you use, e.g. referencing agency, deposit scheme.]

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
