## 1. WHY THE MESSAGE CHANGED

We used to say: *prove your results to investors, stop sending screenshots.*

One of the team used that line to a room of traders and got laughed at — the response was
"as a trader you don't need an investor to start trading." They were right. Almost nobody
in this audience is raising money. We were selling a solution to a problem they don't have.

What they actually want: **to see their own trading clearly and get better at it.** The
calendar showing green and red days. The equity curve. Knowing their real win rate instead
of a vibe. The product always did this; the marketing was pointed somewhere else.

So the new angle is *clarity and improvement*, not *proof and credibility*.

## 3. THE TWO THINGS WORTH SHOUTING ABOUT

Both of these remove a specific reason people were saying no. They matter more than
anything aesthetic we could make.

### a) You don't have to type your trades in

You can now import your whole history from a CSV: **Bybit, MEXC, Binance, MT4, MT5,
cTrader.** "I'm not manually logging every trade" was a completely fair reason not to
bother trying us, and it's gone.

### b) You can do it all from your phone

Building your page used to need a laptop. It doesn't any more — set up, publish, log
trades, import history, all from a phone. Most people see our posts on a phone, so this
closes the gap between "that looks good" and "I'll do it later" (which means never).

**Important on (a):** write the exchange names as **plain text**, not logos. Using their
logos implies a partnership we don't have, and it's a trademark problem. Text names only.

## 6. RULES (unchanged, all still apply)

- **No invented numbers.** No "1,000+ traders", no fake win rates, no made-up P&L. If you
  need a number for a layout, use one from the list in section 4.
- **Sample data must look like sample data.** Realistic but obviously illustrative — and
  it must include losing days. An all-green calendar or a straight-up equity curve reads
  as fake to a trader instantly, and it makes us look like the thing we're against.
- **Never real users' names, handles, photos or numbers.** First names only, and only with
  their say-so.
- **Both markets.** Right now everything skews forex. Please make roughly half the sample
  pairs crypto (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT) and half forex (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY).
  We're pitching both audiences and the graphics currently only speak to one.
- Spelling: **Porfilr** / porfilr.com — you've been getting this right, thank you.
