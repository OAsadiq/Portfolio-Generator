## The five pieces

### 1. Free to start
The headline of the week.

> **15 trades free.**
> Calendar, stats, equity curve, imports — all of it.
> Then $35 once. No subscription.

Lead with what they get. The limit is the second line, not the first.

### 2. The calendar
Our best-looking feature and we've never made a graphic of it. A month grid, days coloured
green and red by profit or loss, trade count and W/L on each day.

Use **made-up numbers**, not a real user's. Make them plausible — a mixed month with a
couple of red days, not a fantasy run of green.

## Standing rules

- **Never the word "verified", and no investor framing.** Numbers are self-reported and the
  product says so. We don't dress that up — being straight about it is the point.
- **No real user data** — no names, no real P&L, no real screenshots of someone's account.
- Every claim here is something the product actually does today. If a line feels stronger
  than the truth, send it to me instead of shipping it.
