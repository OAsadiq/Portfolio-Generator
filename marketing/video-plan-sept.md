# Video Plan — September

Rose asked for two things: a video showing people how to use the site generally, and one
covering the Porfilr Journal in detail. Both are right. One change to how we do the second.

**First, a retirement:** `video-brief-live-track-record.md` is dead. It's built on the old
investor/"stop sending screenshots" angle and calls the product the Trader Kit. Don't shoot
it, and don't reuse its script.

---

## The one change I'd make

"Cover everything in one video" sounds thorough and almost never works. A fifteen-minute
walkthrough gets watched for ninety seconds, can't be posted anywhere, and goes stale the
moment we ship a change — and we ship most weeks.

Same content, two shapes instead:

- **Short task videos** (30–60s each) — one job per video. Postable, searchable, and when a
  feature changes we reshoot one video instead of the whole thing.
- **One long walkthrough** (6–8 min) that lives on the site and in the welcome email, not
  on social. This is the "touch everything" video Rose means, put where a long video
  actually gets watched: in front of someone who has already signed up.

So: same coverage, but the short ones do the reach and the long one does the teaching.

## Track 1 — "What Porfilr is" (1 video, 90s)

The general site video. For someone who has never heard of us.

Not a feature list. Answer three questions in order:

1. **What is it?** You have work worth showing and nowhere good to show it. Pick a template,
   fill in a form, you have a real page in about ten minutes.
2. **What does it look like?** Real pages, scrolling. No mockups.
3. **What does it cost?** Free to start. Pro is $19 once for a custom domain and analytics —
   one time, not a subscription.

Mention the Journal once, near the end, as "and if you trade, there's a version built
around a trading journal" — then stop. Don't let it take over; this video is for everybody.

**Deliverables:** 90s master (16:9 for the site) + a 45s cut (9:16, captions burned in).

## Track 2 — the Porfilr Journal

### 2a. Five short videos, 30–60s each

Shoot these on a phone as well as desktop — most of the audience is on a phone, and we now
support that end to end. One job per video, said in the first three seconds.

1. **Start free.** Sign up → journal open → first trade logged. End on the trade appearing
   in the list. State the limit plainly: 15 trades free, everything included.
2. **Import your history.** Export from Bybit / MEXC / Binance / MT4 / MT5 / cTrader, drop
   the file in, watch the trades populate. Name all six platforms on screen — people search
   for their own broker.
3. **Read your calendar.** The month filling in, green and red days, trade count and W/L on
   each. Least explaining needed, most striking to look at.
4. **Your stats, without a spreadsheet.** Win rate, average win vs average loss, drawdown,
   equity curve. Where the numbers come from and what they mean.
5. **Publish your page.** Turn on the live track record, page updates, done. This is the
   *last* video, not the first — the page is the sidenote now.

### 2b. One full walkthrough, 6–8 min

Everything, in the order a real person hits it: sign up → set starting balance → log a
trade → import history → read the calendar → read the stats → notes and screenshots on a
trade → publish → what happens at trade 15.

Chaptered, so someone can jump. Lives on `/trading-journal` and in the welcome email — not
on social.

## Rules for every video

- **Real screen recordings.** Nothing mocked up. I'll send footage or access.
- **Never say "verified", "synced", or "connected to your broker".** Traders enter their own
  numbers and the page says so plainly. That honesty is the pitch — don't upgrade it.
- **No real user data.** Use a demo account. If a real trade or name is on screen, it doesn't
  ship.
- **Captions on everything.** Most of this is watched muted.
- **Dark and calm.** Bloomberg terminal, not hype trader. Gold accent. No lambos, no cash,
  no neon.
- **Spelling:** Porfilr. One CTA, at the end only: `porfilr.com/trading-journal`.
- Don't say "$35" without "one time, no subscription" next to it.
