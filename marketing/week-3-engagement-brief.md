## The change: this week is engagement only

**No scheduled posting this week.** Seriously — skip it. Every hour goes into conversations.

Here's why: posting into a small audience reaches almost nobody. Replying inside *other
people's* threads — where traders already are — is how we borrow their reach. That's the
only thing that moves us right now.

**Each day:**
- **20+ genuine replies** (up from 10) on threads from bigger accounts our traders follow.
  Real, useful replies. Add something. Never "🔥" or a link drop.
- Go where traders actually talk: prop-firm chatter, funded-trader threads, people posting
  P&L screenshots, anyone complaining about proving their results or getting funded.
- **DM 5 traders** you've had a genuine back-and-forth with. Not a pitch — a real
  conversation. Ask what they use to show their results to investors. Listen.

**Why the DMs matter most:** 42 anonymous visitors are worth less than 5 real conversations.
Those 5 people can tell us what actually stops them — and I can follow up personally.
