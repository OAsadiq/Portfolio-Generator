## What's actually new

**The Trader Kit is now the Porfilr Journal.** Name, page, links — all of it. The old
`/trader-kit` URL redirects, so nothing you've already posted is broken.

That's not just a rename. The pitch is inverted:

- **Before:** a page to show investors your trades
- **Now:** a place to log and read your own trading — the public page is the sidenote

**It's free to start.** 15 trades, everything included: calendar, stats, equity curve,
imports. After 15 you unlock the full journal for $35 once. No subscription, no trial
expiry, no card needed to start.

**It works on a phone.** Build the page, publish it, log trades, import history — all of
it, from a phone. Until recently the builder told mobile users to come back on a laptop.
That was costing us people who never came back.

**Imports:** Bybit, MEXC, Binance, MT4, MT5, cTrader. Export closed positions, drop the
file in.

**The calendar is live.** Day by day, coloured by profit or loss, with trade count and
wins/losses on each day.
