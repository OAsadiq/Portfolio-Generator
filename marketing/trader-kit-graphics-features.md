# Graphics Brief — Trader Kit (feature-focused)

One graphic per feature. Each should make a trader instantly picture the benefit.
Formats: **1:1 (feed)** + **4:5 (portrait)**. Deliver a matching set — same style across all,
so they read as one campaign.

**Look & feel:** dark, premium, "serious money" — Bloomberg terminal, not hype-trader.
Accent **gold (#e0b252)**, green for gains, red for losses, on near-black. Bold clean sans
(Inter). Always include the Porfilr logo + porfilr.com. Spelling: **Porfilr**.

**Honesty rule (must follow):** never show or imply "Verified by Porfilr" or "synced from
your broker." Traders enter their own numbers; the page says they're self-reported. Don't
fake it — honesty is part of the pitch.

---

## The graphics (one per feature)

**1. Live, self-updating track record** — *the flagship*
- Visual: the track-record card with a big green return, and a small pulsing **"Updated 2 days ago"** badge.
- Headline: *A track record that updates itself.*
- Sub: *Log a trade — your page reflects it. Something a screenshot can never do.*

**2. Auto-calculated metrics**
- Visual: the metric grid — total return, win rate, profit factor, max drawdown, trades — with the little fill bars.
- Headline: *Your numbers, worked out for you.*
- Sub: *Return, win rate, drawdown, profit factor — from your logged trades.*

**3. Live equity curve**
- Visual: the gold/green equity curve rising, gridlines, glowing endpoint. "$25,000 → $60,650."
- Headline: *Your equity curve, drawn from real trades.*

**4. "Stop sending screenshots"** — *the hook piece*
- Visual: a messy cropped P&L screenshot in a DM, crossed out → a clean track-record page beside it.
- Headline: *Stop sending screenshots.*
- Sub: *Screenshots are easy to fake. Investors know it.*

**5. Trade journal**
- Visual: the journal — a trade being logged (symbol, side, P&L) with the page updating alongside.
- Headline: *Log your trades. We do the rest.*

**6. CSV import**
- Visual: a broker CSV file dropping in → dozens of trades populating the journal at once.
- Headline: *Import your whole history in one go.*
- Sub: *Export from MT4/MT5 or your broker, drop it in, done.*

**7. Built-in contact form**
- Visual: the page with a "New message" notification — an investor reaching out.
- Headline: *Turn your track record into deal flow.*
- Sub: *Investors and clients reach you directly.*

**8. Built in 10 minutes**
- Visual: before (blank/nothing) → after (the finished trader page), with a small "~10 min" tag.
- Headline: *A page that gets you taken seriously — in 10 minutes.*

## Priority (build first)
1. #4 "Stop sending screenshots" (the hook)
2. #1 Live track record (the flagship feature)
3. #3 Equity curve (most visual)
4. #7 Deal flow (the differentiator)
Then the rest.

## Also useful
- A single **finished trader-page mockup** (hero + metrics + curve + "work with me") — doubles as the strongest promo image and the template thumbnail.

## Do NOT
- No "verified/synced" claims (see honesty rule).
- No invented numbers ("$50k in 3 days") — use realistic demo figures.
- No lambos, cash stacks, neon. That's the look we're beating.
