## HOW TO READ THIS

**Anything inside "quote marks" is the literal text to typeset.** Do not include the label
before it. Last round `Headline:` and `Sub:` got set as visible text — that's my fault for
writing the brief ambiguously, so this time:

> ✅ Set this: **Import your history. Don't type it.**
> ❌ Not this: `Headline: "Import your history. Don't type it."`

If a line looks like an instruction rather than something a trader would read, it's an
instruction. When unsure, ask before setting it.

**Sizes for every asset:** 1080×1350 (portrait — our best reach) as the master, plus a
1080×1080 square crop. Stories/Reels 1080×1920 only where noted.

## THE SAMPLE ACCOUNT — use these numbers everywhere

One fictional account across the whole set, so the graphics read as one system instead of
a pile of unrelated screenshots. **Never invent different numbers** — if you need a figure
that isn't here, ask me.

```
Starting balance     $5,000
Month                18 trading days
Net                  +$742
Win rate             58%
Trades               31
Best day             +$310
Worst day            -$198
Max drawdown         -$412
Profit factor        1.6
```

**Why these numbers and not better ones:** a 58% win rate with a real drawdown is what a
competent trader's month looks like. A 90% win rate and a straight line up reads as fake
to this audience in about one second, and it makes us look like the screenshot-flexers
we're positioned against. The drawdown is a feature of the graphic, not a flaw.

**Every graphic showing sample data carries this in small text, bottom corner:**
"Sample data"

**Pairs — mix both markets across the set, roughly half and half:**
Crypto: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT · Forex: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY

## ASSET 1 — Hero: "Import your history"

*Priority piece. This one removes the biggest objection we have.*

**Layout:** left-to-right transformation. Left third: a plain CSV file icon, deliberately
boring, labelled "bybit-export.csv". Middle: a thin arrow or motion line. Right two-thirds:
the journal calendar and equity curve, in colour, alive.

The story is the transformation — dull file in, clear picture out. Give the right side
roughly twice the visual weight of the left.

**Typeset:**

> **Import your history. Don't type it.**
>
> Bybit · MEXC · Binance · MT4 · MT5 · cTrader

Small line under, optional:

> Months of trades in about a minute.

**Text, not logos** for the exchange names — logos imply a partnership we don't have.

**If you have time:** a 3-second loop of the file dropping and the calendar filling in is
worth more than any static version of this.
