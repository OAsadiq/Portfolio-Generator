## The one thing that must be right

There are two kinds of export and only one of them works:

- **Closed positions** — one row per completed trade, with the profit on it. This is what
  we need.
- **Fills** — every individual buy and sell listed separately. No profit per trade, so
  there's nothing to import.

On **Bybit** the right one is called **"Closed P&L"**. On **MEXC and Binance** it's the
futures position/trade history that includes **realised P&L**.

We detect a fills file and say so plainly rather than importing nonsense — worth showing,
see the script.
