## ROLE 1 — Running the Porfilr accounts (X + Instagram)

**Your job in one line: be the voice of Porfilr — create awareness, start conversations,
and take care of everyone who talks to us.**

**1. Create awareness.**
Post daily about what we're building (right now: the Trader Kit and the 20 founding spots).
Shed light on what Porfilr is and why it exists — the screenshot problem, the live track
record, the story of what we're doing. Words lead on X, visuals lead on Instagram.

**2. Start conversations.**
Don't just broadcast — ask questions in posts, jump into relevant threads (10+ genuine
replies a day), and get people talking about proving your work / proving your results.
A post that starts 5 real conversations beats one that gets 50 quiet likes.

**3. Respond to everything directed at us.** *(This part is new and important.)*
You're now our front door. Replies to our posts, questions in DMs, people asking how
something works — answer them warmly and fast. Nobody who talks to Porfilr should feel
ignored.
- Product questions you know the answer to → answer directly.
- Anything about **pricing deals, refunds, bugs, partnerships, or a complaint** → don't
  improvise; forward it to me and tell the person "let me get you the right answer."
- Someone seriously interested in the Trader Kit → point them to porfilr.com/trader-kit,
  and flag them to me.

**Your link:** porfilr.com/r/c1 — bio + wherever it fits naturally.

**Friday update:** what you posted, conversations started, questions/DMs handled, follower
growth, anything you noticed.

## ROLE 2 — Driving from your own accounts + spotting leads

**Your job in one line: pull people toward Porfilr from your own audience — and when you
find a real potential trader, hand them to me so I can close them personally.**

**1. Create pull.**
Talk about the Trader Kit and the founding offer in your own voice to your audience.
Engage 10+ relevant people a day. Point interested people to your link.

**2. Watch for leads.** *(This part is new and important.)*
While you're engaging, keep an eye out for traders who look like real prospects:
- Complaining about proving their results / sending screenshots
- Selling signals or mentorship, or trying to get funded
- Asking questions about the kit, or engaging with you about it more than once

**3. Relay leads to me — don't close them yourself.**
When you spot one, warm them up naturally (a good conversation, not a pitch), then send me:

> **Lead:** @handle — link to the conversation — one line on why they're a fit / what they said.

Send them as you find them, or a small batch each day. I'll DM them personally as the
founder and tell them about the kit directly — that lands far better coming from me.
Your job is finding and warming them; mine is closing. Don't promise them anything
(pricing, features, timelines) — just get them curious and pass them over.

**Your link:** porfilr.com/r/c2 — bio + wherever it fits naturally.

**Friday update:** what you posted, who you engaged, **leads relayed (this is your headline
number now)**, clicks/interest you noticed.
