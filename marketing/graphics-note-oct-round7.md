# Graphics — round 7, the payment update

Round 6 was about what the Journal *tells* you. This round is about something more
practical: **people can now actually pay us.**

Same dark/gold direction. Four pieces and one small update.

**Deadline: Friday 23 October.** If only some land, do #1 — it carries the round.

---

## What changed

Until recently the only way to pay was a card in dollars. Plenty of traders here simply
cannot be charged in dollars — the card declines and that's the end of it. So the biggest
reason people didn't buy had nothing to do with the product.

Now there are three ways to pay: **card, bank transfer in naira, and USDT.**

And it's a **one-time payment**, not a subscription. That matters as much as the naira does.

---

## No prices on anything

Deliberate. Prices move, graphics don't — and a graphic showing the wrong number is money
someone transfers incorrectly. The site always has the current figure, so these point
people there instead of competing with it.

Say "one time" and "free to start". Never a number, never a dollar conversion.

## The pieces

### 1. How you can pay — the main one
One graphic, three options, equal weight. No hierarchy between them: for the person who
needs bank transfer, that IS the main option.

> **Three ways to pay.**
> Card · Bank transfer (naira) · USDT

Underneath, smaller:

> *One time. Not a subscription.*
> *Free for your first 15 trades.*

Keep it calm and legible — this is a reference card people screenshot and send to someone
else, not an advert. Three clean marks or labels, nothing crowded.

### 2. One time, not monthly
The pricing *shape*, with no number in it.

> **Pay once.**
> *Not every month.*

Second line smaller and quieter. The whole effect is the contrast between the two.

### 3. The honest answer
For threads where someone asks why use this over apps they've heard of. Should read like a
person talking, not a poster.

> *"There are plenty of trading journals.*
> *Most of them you can't actually buy here — monthly, dollars, card only.*
> **This one you pay once, and you can pay in naira."**

Plain type. No icons, no badges, no competitor names. The honesty is the design.

### 4. The line
One clean card for everywhere — end of videos, profile, pinned post. Should look settled,
not like a campaign.

> **Log your trades.**
> **See what they're telling you.**

Nothing else on it but the logo.

### 5. Update the reply card from round 6
It currently reads *"Free for your first 15 · porfilr.com/trading-journal."* Add the payment
point, since that's the part people don't know:

> Log your trades. See what they're telling you.
> Free for your first 15 · card, transfer or USDT

Still has to work at thumbnail size with no caption — it gets dropped into other people's
comment sections. Same gate as #1: holds until crypto is live.
