## 3. The Trader Kit — in detail

**The problem it solves.** Traders prove their results by sending screenshots of their
profit. Screenshots are trivially faked, and investors know it — so honest traders get lumped
in with the fakes and ignored.

**What a trader gets:**
1. **A professional trader page** — bio, markets, strategy, risk approach, and what they
   offer (managed accounts, signals, mentorship).
2. **A private trade journal** — they log each trade (symbol, entry, exit, P&L), or import
   their whole history at once from a broker CSV (MT4/MT5 etc.).
3. **A live track record** — the page calculates and displays their real total return, win
   rate, profit factor, max drawdown and number of trades, **automatically from their logged
   trades.**
4. **A live equity curve** that draws itself from their real data.
5. **A trading calendar** — a month view where each day is coloured by profit or loss, so
   they can see their own consistency and patterns. Private by default; they can choose to
   show it publicly.
6. **The page updates itself** — log a trade, and the public page reflects it, including a
   "last updated" date.
7. **A built-in contact form** so investors and clients reach them directly.
8. Custom domain, analytics, and no Porfilr branding are all included.

**One-sentence version:** *The Trader Kit gives traders a professional page where their
logged trades become a live track record — return, win rate, drawdown, equity curve — plus a
journal and calendar showing their wins and losses day by day, so they can both prove their
results to investors and learn from their own patterns to improve.*

**Positioning line:** *MyFXBook is your proof. Porfilr is your pitch.*

### ⚠️ The honesty rule — this one is absolute

**We never say a trader's results are "verified" by us, or synced from their broker.**
Traders enter their own numbers, and the page states plainly that they're self-reported.

Never write "verified", "auto-synced", "broker-verified", or anything implying we check the
data. It's a legal and trust line. In a space full of hype, being the honest one is our
actual advantage — and traders notice.

## 5. The referral programme (for our users)

Users can refer friends and earn rewards. How it works:

- Every user gets a referral link from their dashboard
- A referral "qualifies" when the friend **buys Pro**
- **3 qualifying referrals → they get Pro free**
- Once they're Pro, **the next qualifying referral → 1 free kit** (capped at one, ever)

So the ladder is: free user gets Pro at referral #3, then a free kit at #4. Worth mentioning
in content — it gives existing users a reason to spread the word.
