## THE SAMPLE MONTH — use these numbers everywhere

Every figure below comes from one real calculation, so the calendar, the curve and the
stats all agree. **Do not adjust individual numbers to fill a layout** — a trader adds these
up, and if the win rate doesn't match the wins and losses we've lost them.

```
Starting balance     $10,000
Trades               18   (10 wins, 8 losses)
Win rate             55.6%
Net P&L              +$899
Return               +8.99%
Profit factor        1.92
Max drawdown         5.78%
Trading days         18   (10 green, 8 red)

By week:   W1 +275     W2 −40     W3 +360     W4 +304
```
