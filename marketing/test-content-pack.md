## SHARED — read this first (both candidates)

**What this is about:** the **Trader Kit** — a Porfilr product for traders. Instead of
sending screenshots to prove their results, a trader gets a real page that turns their
logged trades into a live, credible track record.

**The goal of these 3 days: awareness, not selling.** We haven't fully launched the Trader
Kit yet. The job right now is to warm people up — get traders (and the wider audience)
aware this is coming and interested. Think "start the conversation," not "buy now."

**About the graphics:** I'll send you graphics you can attach to posts. Use them as
*support*, not the main event — **especially on X, where the words and the conversation
matter far more than the image.** On Instagram it's the opposite: the visual leads.

**The one link:** porfilr.com — keep it in the bio, drop it into replies only when it fits
naturally. Don't spam it.

**The topic — everything you post/talk about should orbit this:**
- Traders "prove" results with screenshots, and screenshots are easy to fake
- Good traders get lumped in with fake ones — no credible way to stand out
- What if your track record was a real, live page instead of a screenshot?
- Getting funded, landing investors/clients — credibility is everything
- "MyFXBook is your proof. Porfilr is your pitch."

**Post angles you can use (write them in your own words):**
- *Traders — how are you actually proving your results to people? Screenshots? Be honest 👇*
- *A screenshot is a moment. A real track record is proof. Which one are you sending investors?*
- *Anyone can fake a P&L screenshot. That's exactly why the good traders get ignored with the bad ones.*
- *We're building something for traders who are tired of not being taken seriously. More soon 👀*

**Honesty rule (important):** never say results are "verified" by us or synced from a broker.
Traders enter their own numbers and the page says so. Don't overclaim it — being honest is
part of the pitch.

**What to send me at the end (both of you):**
1. What you posted and where
2. Who you engaged with — roughly how many a day, what kind of people
3. What you noticed — what got replies, what fell flat, what you'd try next
4. Any signals — profile visits, link clicks, people showing real interest

Don't stress about big numbers in 3 days. Show me how you connect with people.

**Your test: post from our accounts AND drive engagement.**

You'll be posting from our **Porfilr X account and our Instagram**, plus engaging. Two things

**1. Posting.** Put out the awareness content above across the 3 days (~1–2 posts a day).

**2. Engaging.** Each day, reply to and genuinely engage with **at least 10 relevant people**
— traders, and the wider creator audience. Real replies that add something, not "🔥". Start
conversations that make people curious about what we're building.

**Please, since it's our account:** don't change the profile, bio, or handle, and don't post
anything that commits us (pricing, launch dates, partnerships) without checking with me.
