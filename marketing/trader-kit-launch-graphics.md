# Graphics Brief — Trader Kit LAUNCH set (batch 2)

The first batch covers individual features. This batch is the **announcement** — the pieces
that say "this exists, it's here, here's what it is." These get posted first and pinned, so
they matter most.

**Same look as batch 1:** dark, premium, "serious money." Gold **#e0b252**, green for gains,
red for losses, near-black background. Bold clean sans (Inter). Logo + porfilr.com on every
piece. Spelling: **Porfilr**.

**Formats:** 1:1 (feed) + 4:5 (portrait). Plus the two banner sizes noted below.

**Honesty rule (unchanged):** never show "Verified by Porfilr" or imply we verify/sync
results. Traders enter their own numbers and the page says so.

---

## 1. THE LAUNCH PIECE — "The Trader Kit is here" ⭐ *most important*
The hero announcement. One image that shows the product and says it's live.
- **Visual:** the finished trader page (hero + metrics card + equity curve) shown on a

- **Headline:** *The Trader Kit is here.*
- **Sub:** *A track record that updates itself — not a screenshot.*
- **Tag:** porfilr.com/trader-kit
- This is the pinned post, the launch tweet image, and the thumbnail everywhere.

## 2. Founding offer — "20 spots"
Scarcity piece for the founding push.
- **Visual:** bold "20" as the focal point, or a subtle 20-block grid with a few filled gold.
- **Headline:** *20 founding spots.*

  plus optional variants: "Only 10 left" / "Only 5 left" / "Founding closed".

## 3. What you get — the kit at a glance
One image summarising the whole kit (for people who won't read a thread).

- **Headline:** *Everything in the Trader Kit.*
