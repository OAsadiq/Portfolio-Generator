Hi! Third of five. The first two covered getting trades *in* — by hand, then by import.
This one is the first about getting something *out*, and it's the one I most want to land.

**Length:** 45–60s. Rework the script freely. Desktop is fine for the grid (it's easier to

## Why this one matters

Someone found us through a comment, messaged, and asked two questions: *how do you log
trades*, and *how do you read it?*

We've answered the first one twice. **Nothing we've made answers the second.** Every asset
so far shows the calendar as a nice-looking object. None of them shows what a trader
learns from looking at it.

So the job here isn't "we have a calendar". It's **"here's something about your trading you
didn't know."**

---

## What's actually on screen

Real labels, so nothing has to be invented:

- Panel heading: **Trading calendar**
- A month grid, Monday-first, days coloured by profit or loss
- Each traded day shows its **P&L, trade count, wins and losses**
- Below the grid: **"Days with no closed trades are left blank."**
- Month summary: total P&L, **trading days**, **green days**, **red days**, total trades
- A trade lands on the day it **closed** — that's when the money is real
- There's a separate toggle, **"Show my trading calendar on my page"**, for making it public

Use the demo account. Pick a month that's mixed — mostly green with three or four red days
— and make sure there's a visible pattern in it, because the whole video hangs on that.

## Draft script (45–60s) — rework freely

**0–5s — the question**
*Visual:* a list of trades, scrolling. Plain, a bit dull.
*Caption:* **You logged 40 trades. So what?**

**5–12s — the turn**
*Visual:* cut to the calendar filling in, day by day, green and red.
*Caption:* **Same trades. Now you can see them.**

**12–25s — reading a day**
*Visual:* hover or tap one day. It shows the P&L, the number of trades, the wins and losses.
*Caption:* **Every day: what you made, how many trades, how many went your way.**
*Unhurried. One day, read properly, teaches more than the whole grid flashing past.*

**25–40s — the thing they didn't know (the point of the video)**
*Visual:* pull back to the full month. Highlight the red days — a circle, a line, something
simple. They cluster.
*Caption:* **Three of your four losing days were Fridays.**
*Then:* **You don't notice that in your head.**
*This is the beat the video exists for. Give it the most room.*

**40–50s — the month summary**
*Visual:* the summary beside the grid — trading days, green days, red days.
*Caption:* **Your month, in five numbers.**

**50–58s — close**
*Caption:* **Log your trades. See what they're telling you.**
*End card:* **porfilr.com/trading-journal**

## Rules

- Captions on everything. Dark and calm, gold accent. No lambos, cash or neon.
