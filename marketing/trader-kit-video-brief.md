## What we're launching

The **Trader Kit** — a Porfilr product built specifically for traders.

Most traders "prove" their results by sending screenshots in a DM. Screenshots are easy to fake and investors know it, so good traders get lumped in with fake ones. The Trader Kit gives them a real page instead.

## What it actually does

- **A professional trader page** — their photo, bio, markets, strategy, risk approach, and what they offer (managed accounts, signals, mentorship). Built in about 10 minutes, no code.
- **A private trade journal** — they log each trade (or import their whole history from a broker CSV in one go).
- **The page works out their track record automatically** — total return, win rate, profit factor, max drawdown, number of trades.
- **A live equity curve** that draws itself from their real logged trades.
- **The page updates itself.** Log a trade, and their public page reflects it — including a "last updated" stamp. *This is the thing nothing else does, and the most important idea to land.*
- **A built-in contact form** so investors and clients reach them directly.

## Who it's for
Forex, futures, and crypto traders who need to be taken seriously — pitching investors, applying to prop firms, or selling signals/mentorship. Mostly younger, mobile-first, but the page gets sent to people with money.

## The core message
**Stop sending screenshots.** A screenshot is a moment; this is a track record that keeps itself current.

Useful contrast: MyFXBook and broker dashboards are proof, but they're ugly and not something you'd pitch with. **"MyFXBook is your proof. Porfilr is your pitch."**
