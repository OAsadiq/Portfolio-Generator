## The feature: a track record that updates itself

**The problem it solves.** Traders prove their results by sending screenshots of their profit.
Screenshots are trivially faked, and investors know it — so honest traders get lumped in with
the fakes and ignored.

**What Porfilr does.** The trader logs their trades in a private journal. Their public page
then calculates and displays their real track record — total return, win rate, max drawdown,
equity curve — and **updates itself every time they log a new trade.** The page also shows
when it was last updated.

**Why that matters.** A screenshot is a frozen moment that anyone could have edited. A page
that updates itself, with a visible "last updated" date, is something a fake can't maintain.
That's the whole idea: *a screenshot is a moment; this is a track record.*

**One rule that can't be broken:** we never say the results are "verified" by us or synced
from a broker. Traders enter their own numbers, and the page says so plainly. Please don't
add "verified", "auto-synced", or anything implying we check the data. Being honest about
that is deliberately part of our pitch.
