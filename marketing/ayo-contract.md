## 1. What the Contractor will do

The Contractor will provide marketing and growth services for Porfilr, including:

- Promoting Porfilr and the Trader Kit across social media and other channels
- Creating and/or distributing content, and engaging with potential customers
- Driving traffic and sales to porfilr.com
- Advising on marketing strategy and reporting on results

The Contractor decides **how** the work is done, using their own methods, tools and schedule.
The Company sets the objectives, not the working hours.

---

## 2. Payment

### 2.1 Retainer
**$100 (one hundred US dollars) per month**, paid [monthly in arrears / on the Xth of each
month] by [payment method].

### 2.2 Commission
**25% of net revenue from every Trader Kit founding sale the Contractor drives.**

- Applies to the **one-time founding offer** ($35) — approximately **$8.75 per sale**.
- **No commission is payable on monthly subscriptions** under this Agreement. Once the
  founding offer closes and the Trader Kit moves to a monthly price, both parties will
  discuss and agree new commission terms in writing before that change takes effect.
- "Net revenue" means the amount the Company actually receives after payment-processor fees
  (Stripe) and after any refunds or chargebacks.

### 2.3 How sales are attributed
- The Contractor is given a unique tracked link: **porfilr.com/r/ayo**
- A sale is attributed to the Contractor when the customer arrived through that link
- The Company will share attribution data openly and on request; the Contractor does not have
  to rely on the Company's word alone
- Where attribution is genuinely unclear or disputed, both parties will discuss it in good
  faith and agree a resolution

### 2.4 When commission is paid
Commission is calculated monthly and paid within [14] days of the end of each month,
alongside the retainer. Commission on a refunded or charged-back sale is not payable, and if
already paid, is deducted from the next payment.

### 2.5 Expenses
The Contractor covers their own costs (data, tools, devices). Any expense the Company should
reimburse — including paid advertising spend — must be agreed **in writing in advance**.

---

## 3. Status

The Contractor is an **independent contractor**, not an employee, partner, or agent of the
Company. They are responsible for their own taxes, and this Agreement does not create an
employment relationship. The Contractor may work with other clients, provided they do not
work with a direct competitor of Porfilr during this Agreement.

---

## 4. Ownership of work

- Content the Contractor creates **specifically for Porfilr** as paid work (posts, copy,
  graphics, campaigns) belongs to the Company.
- The Contractor's **own social media accounts, audience and personal brand remain entirely
  their own.** Nothing in this Agreement gives the Company any claim over them.
- The Company's brand, product, code, and materials remain the Company's property.
- The Contractor may reference this work in their own portfolio and case studies.

---

## 5. Confidentiality and data

The Contractor will have access to non-public information — revenue figures, user numbers,
strategy, customer details. They agree to:

- Keep it confidential during and after this Agreement
- Not share Porfilr's revenue, user data, or internal numbers publicly without permission
- Never publish a customer's personal details (name, email, portfolio) without that
  customer's consent
- Not use Porfilr's data for any purpose other than this work

If given access to Company accounts, the Contractor will not change profile details,
passwords, or account settings without permission, and will return/relinquish access
immediately when this Agreement ends.

---

## 6. Honest marketing (important to the Company)

The Contractor agrees, in all Porfilr marketing:

1. **Never to state or imply that Porfilr verifies, audits or broker-syncs a trader's
   results.** Traders self-report their figures and the product says so. This is a legal and
   trust boundary.
2. Not to make invented or unsubstantiated claims about earnings, results, or outcomes.
3. Not to commit the Company to pricing, partnerships, launch dates or refunds without the
   Founder's approval.

Breach of clause 6.1 is grounds for immediate termination.

---

## 7. Term and ending the Agreement

- This Agreement runs month to month, starting [date].
- **Either party may end it with 14 days' written notice**, for any reason.
- The Company may end it immediately for a serious breach (clause 5 or 6.1).
- On ending: the Company pays all retainer owed up to the end date, plus all commission
  earned on qualifying sales made **before** the end date.
- Confidentiality (clause 5) survives the end of this Agreement.

---

## 8. Liability

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential losses. The Company's
total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts payable to the Contractor.

  discussions.
- Changes must be agreed in writing (email is fine) by both parties.
- Governed by the laws of [Nigeria / relevant jurisdiction].
- If either party is unhappy with anything here, we talk about it first and in good faith
