# Hiring — Social & Community person (simple version)

Goal: make the role sound doable so more people apply. Plain words, low barrier to apply,
then a small paid task tells us who can actually do it.

---

## The job post (paste this)

**We're looking for someone to help people discover Porfilr on social media.**

Porfilr helps people build a clean, professional portfolio in about 10 minutes — no code,
no design skills. It's live, people are using it, and we have videos and graphics ready to go.
What we need now is someone to get it in front of more people.

**What you'd actually do (day to day):**
- Post our content on X (Twitter), Instagram, and TikTok
- Spend time replying to and chatting with people in our space — freelancers, designers, writers, traders. This part matters most.
- Join conversations and communities where our kind of people hang out
- Keep an eye on what's working and do more of it
- Tell us how it's going each week

**You might be a great fit if you:**
- Enjoy being active on social media and talking to people online
- Are the type to reply, comment, and start conversations — not just post and leave
- Have grown an account before (even a small personal one — just show us)
- Are reliable and can work on your own without being chased

You don't need a marketing degree or years of experience. If you're good with people online
and you're consistent, we want to hear from you.

**The details:**
- Part-time, remote, flexible hours
- Starts with a small **paid test task** so we both see how it goes, then ongoing if it works
- You'll get all our content (videos, graphics, posts) ready to use — you're not starting from scratch

**How to apply:**
Send a message with:
1. A social account you run or helped grow (any size — just share the link)
2. One or two lines on what you'd do first for us
3. What you'd want to be paid

Start your message with the word **"PORFILR"** so I know you read this.

---

## How to screen (for me, not in the post)

Keep it simple. The one thing that actually matters: **do they engage, or do they just post?**
The last hire could make a post but couldn't start conversations or pull people in — that's the
gap to test for.

**The paid test task (send to a shortlist):**
> "Porfilr helps people build a portfolio in 10 minutes, free. Our audience is designers,
> developers, writers, and traders. For 3 days, I'd like you to post our content AND — more
> importantly — reply to and engage with at least 10 relevant people each day. At the end,
> send me a short note: what you posted, who you engaged with, and anything you noticed.
> This is paid ($[X])."

**What good looks like:**
- They actually engaged with real people, not just posted
- They can tell me what they noticed and what they'd try next
- They talk about getting people to the site / signing up, not just likes

**Red flags:**
- Only posted, no real conversations started
- Only talks about likes, followers, "going viral"
- Vague, no specifics

Keep the KPI simple and human when they start: **more visitors to the site, and more people
signing up.** That's it.
