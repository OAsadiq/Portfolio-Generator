Hi! First of five short ones on the Porfilr Journal. Each covers a single job. This one is
the simplest and the most important: **someone signs up and logs their first trade.**

Same offer as last time — I've drafted a script because the trading details are easy to get

---

## What this video has to land

One idea: **you can be logging trades in about two minutes, free, without a card.**

Not "here are our features." The viewer should finish thinking *that looked easy* and
nothing else.

Three facts, all true, none negotiable:

- **Free to start — 15 trades, everything included.** Calendar, stats, equity curve, CSV
  imports. Nothing is held back on the free tier except the number of trades.
- **No card to sign up.** Email and a code. That's the whole signup.
- **Past 15 it's $35, one time, no subscription.**

**Say the cap as "your first 15 trades are free"** — never "we keep your most recent 15."
Nothing is ever deleted. Past 15 you simply can't add more until you unlock, and everything
already logged stays exactly where it is. (Our own app currently words this badly; I'm
fixing it. Go with the wording here.)

## Draft script (45–60s) — rework freely

**0–3s — the hook**
*Visual:* a phone, thumb hovering over a trading app. Cut to a hand-scrawled trade in a
paper notebook, or a messy spreadsheet.
*Caption:* **Still logging trades in a notes app?**

**3–10s — signup**
*Visual:* porfilr.com/trading-journal on the phone. Tap **Start free**. Type an email. A
six-digit code arrives, gets typed in. Straight through to the journal.
*Caption:* **Email. Code. In.**
*Then, small:* **No card.**
*Keep this tight — the point is how little there is.*

**10–18s — starting balance**
*Visual:* the **Starting balance** field. Type `10000`. It saves.
*Caption:* **Set your starting balance once.**
*Then:* **Everything measures against it.**

**18–35s — the first trade (the core)**
*Visual:* the trade form filling in, field by field, unhurried:
`EURUSD` · Direction · Entry `1.0850` · Exit `1.0920` · P&L `120.50`
Then save. The trade appears in the list.
*Caption:* **Log a closed trade.**
*Then, as it saves:* **That's it.**
*Let the save land. Don't rush the cut — this is the moment the product becomes real.*

**35–45s — what it becomes**
*Visual:* cut to a journal that already has ~40 trades. The calendar with green and red
days. The equity curve. Scroll once, slowly.
*Caption:* **Do that a few times and you get this.**
*Then:* **Calendar. Win rate. Drawdown. Your whole curve.**
*This is the payoff shot. Give it the most screen time of anything here.*

**45–55s — the offer + close**
*Caption:* **Your first 15 trades are free.**
*Then:* **Everything included — nothing held back.**
*End card, gold on dark:* **porfilr.com/trading-journal**
