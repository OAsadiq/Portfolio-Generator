## The roles — who does what this month

### Growth hire 1 (Porfilr X + Instagram) — building the channel
- **Job:** post Trader Kit awareness content daily (words-first on X, visual-first on IG) +
  engage 10+ relevant people/day. Build followers and presence from near-zero.
- **Uses:** porfilr.com/r/c1 only.
- **Judged on:** follower growth, real conversations, consistency. NOT signups (unfair at zero reach).
- **Owes you:** short update every Friday.
- **You owe her:** account access, weekly content drops, prompt answers, pay on time.

### Growth hire 2 (her own X + Instagram) — driving traffic now
- **Job:** talk about the Trader Kit in her own voice to her existing base + engage 10+
  relevant people/day, pointing interested people to the app.
- **Uses:** porfilr.com/r/c2 only.
- **Judged on:** clicks and signups — engagement that leads somewhere.
- **Owes you:** short update every Friday.
- **You owe her:** graphics/content weekly, freedom in approach, pay on time.

### Video editor — the launch video
- **Deliverable:** hero video (30–45s) + 3 short cuts (10–15s), 9:16 + 1:1, captioned +
  clean versions. She writes her own script.
- **Checkpoint:** script/direction to you for approval BEFORE the full edit (week 1).
- **Target:** hero video done by end of week 2, cuts by week 3 — so the growth hires have
  fresh ammunition for the back half of the month.
- **Hard rules:** real screen recordings only; never "verified/synced" claims; no hype aesthetic.

### Graphics designer — the feature set
- **Deliverable:** the 8 feature graphics (trader-kit-graphics-features.md), 1:1 + 4:5,
  one matched style.
- **Priority order:** #4 Stop sending screenshots → #1 Live track record → #3 Equity curve →
  #7 Deal flow → rest. First two by end of week 1 if possible — the hires need them.
- **Bonus:** the finished trader-page mockup (doubles as promo + template thumbnail).

### Motion designer — HOLD (honest call)
Only if budget comfortably allows. The video editor's screen recordings cover the launch;
paying two video people for overlapping work is the kind of spend to avoid this month.
If already committed/cheap: give ONE contained task — an animated version of the equity-curve
graphic (10s loop) for IG. Otherwise park until kit revenue funds it.

### You (the founder) — the highest-converting channel
- [ ] **5–10 personal DMs/week** to warm traders (the ones who asked + new ones the hires surface)
- [ ] **One personal distribution moment per week** — a Space, a build-in-public thread, the
      founder story. End each with the founding offer.
- [ ] Approve the video script (week 1), review graphics as they land
- [ ] **Friday: pull the scorecard**, reply to both hires' updates, adjust
- [ ] Build nothing except what real users hit

## Week-by-week rhythm

**Week 1 — Launch the engine.** Hires start Monday. First graphics land. Video script approved.
You: DM the warm list, one thread/Space. Target: first 3–5 founding sales.

**Week 2 — First data.** Hero video lands and both hires push it. First scorecard comparison
(c1 vs c2 clicks). You: keep DMs going; ask the first buyers for a testimonial line.
Target: ~8–10 total spots.

**Week 3 — Double down.** Cut what's flat, feed what's moving (you'll know by now which posts
and which hire drive clicks). Short cuts go out. Post first testimonial/founding-trader page
(permission, first name). Target: ~14 spots.

**Week 4 — Close the cohort.** "X spots left" content is now real scarcity — use it.
Final push: DMs to everyone who clicked but didn't buy (the email capture list).
Review month: keep/cut decisions on each role, funded by what the month earned.
Target: 20 — flip to $15/mo.

---

## If it's not working (say it early, not at week 4)
- Under ~5 sales by end of week 2 → the message or the audience is off, not the effort.
  Get on calls with 3 traders who clicked but didn't buy and ask why. Fix that one thing.
- Zero movement from a hire for 2 straight weeks → restructure or stop that lane. Pay for
  outcomes you can see in the scorecard, not activity.
