# Growth Brief — posting on the founder's account (@oasadiq)

You now have access to post on my account. That's a big trust step — this brief is so you can move fast without checking every decision with me.

**The one goal:** get people to porfilr.com and signed up. Not likes. Not impressions.

---

## Who we're for
Freelancers and creators whose work is real but invisible — designers, developers, writers, photographers, and right now especially **traders**. The person who does good work but loses the job/client to someone who simply presents better.

**Our line:** *"Your work is the proof. Porfilr makes it visible."*
Free to start. A professional portfolio in ~10 minutes, no code.

---

## Who our competitors are (you asked)

**Portfolio builders — Carrd, Framer, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, Behance, Dribbble, Notion pages.**
Why we win: they're either *design tools* (you still have to be a designer and spend a weekend) or *galleries you don't own* (Behance/Dribbble — your work sits inside their brand, competing with everyone else's). Porfilr is 10 minutes, no design skill, and the page is yours.

**For traders specifically — MyFXBook, FXBlue, prop-firm dashboards.**
Why we win: those are *verification tools* — accurate but ugly, and not something you'd send an investor as a pitch. Our line for that audience: **"MyFXBook is your proof. Porfilr is your pitch."** Nobody offers the credible, beautiful, shareable layer on top.

**The real competitor is doing nothing** — the screenshot in a DM, the "let me put something together and get back to you." Most people don't have a bad portfolio; they have *none*. That's who we're actually taking share from, and it's who our content should speak to.

---

## How to run the account

**Engagement is the job, not posting.** Our reach problem doesn't get fixed by better posts — it gets fixed by showing up where the audience already is. Before writing anything of our own, spend the first block of each day leaving **10–15 genuine, useful replies** on threads from bigger accounts our audience follows (job-seekers, freelancers, designers, devs, traders). That's what actually pulls people back to the profile.

**Cadence:** 1–2 posts a day. Mix roughly 4 value posts : 1 about Porfilr. Never let the timeline read like an ad feed.

**What works for us:**
- Concrete pain → practical fix (the stuff you're already doing well)
- Short lessons from building in public — the honest version, including what isn't working
- Before/after: messy screenshots vs a real page
- Trader angle: track records, credibility, getting funded

**Always leave a door open.** Not salesy, but bio + link current, and every so often a natural "here's what I'm building and why."

## Metrics that matter
Report these, not likes/impressions:
1. **Profile visits**
2. **Link clicks**
3. **New followers**
4. **Replies you started**
5. **Signups** (I'll pull these)

## Voice
First person, as me. Plain and direct — no corporate speak, no hype, no emoji spam. Confident but honest: we're early and building in the open, and that's a strength.

## Don't
- Don't promise results we can't back ("get hired in 7 days").
- Don't claim trader results are verified by us — they're self-reported, and our product says so. Honesty is our edge; never trade it for a punchier line.
- Don't argue with anyone or touch politics/religion from my account.
- Don't post other users' names, emails, or portfolios without asking me.
- Don't change bio, handle, or profile/banner images without checking first.
- Check with me before anything that commits us — pricing, partnerships, launch dates.
